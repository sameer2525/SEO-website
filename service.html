05 / FAQs

Before we
get started.

Have a question about the services or project scope? Here are a few useful starting points.

Ask about your project ↗
Can I choose just one service? +

Yes. You can enquire about one specific service or discuss a combination of services, depending on your goals and requirements.

Do you offer SEO for e-commerce websites? +

Yes. SEO support can include product and category page optimization, keyword research, technical reviews, internal linking and Google Merchant Center product-content reviews where relevant.

Can you help with both design and video? +

Graphic design and video editing are separate service areas. Depending on the brief, they can also support the same social media or advertising campaign.

How is the project scope decided? +

The scope is based on the requirements, current setup, expected deliverables, access, timeline and budget. These details should be discussed before work begins.

Can you guarantee rankings or leads? +

No specific ranking, lead volume or advertising result can be guaranteed. Performance depends on many factors, including competition, budget, website quality, offer and tracking.
